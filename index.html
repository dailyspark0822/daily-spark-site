Common questions

When is my episode ready?

New episodes are made overnight and are ready by about 4:00 AM Pacific time. You can turn on a morning reminder in Account → Morning reminder and pick the time.

How do I change my topics?

Go to Account → Your topics → Edit. Changes apply to your next morning's episode.

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 3 days free, then $9.99 per month. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Daily Spark → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Account screen. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

Sign in with the same Daily Spark account, then tap Restore purchases in the Account screen. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

Why can I only see today's and yesterday's episodes?

Daily Spark is about what's new. To protect your privacy and keep the service affordable, episodes are deleted after two days.

Who are Maya and Jordan?

They're AI voices. Each episode is researched, written and voiced by AI from the news sources listed with it. AI can make mistakes – tap Transcript → Sources to see where each story came from, and let us know if something's off.

I didn't get an episode today.

Pull down on the episode screen to refresh. Brand-new accounts get their first episode the next morning. If you haven't listened to or opened the app for your last few episodes, we pause new ones to save resources – just open the app and they'll start again the next morning. Still missing? Email us.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, topics and episodes. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that in Settings first.
