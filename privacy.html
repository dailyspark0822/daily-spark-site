Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Daily Spark is an iPhone app that turns the news on topics you choose into a short personal podcast each morning. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version

  • We collect only what's needed to make your daily episode: your email, your topics, and whether you listened.
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Episodes are kept for two days and then deleted.
  • You can delete your account and everything with it, anytime, in the app.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Email address and password (the password is stored only in a scrambled, unreadable form)To create your account and sign you in.
Your topics (up to 4)To research the news and write your episode.
Your episodes (audio, transcript and the list of news sources)So you can listen and see where each story came from.
Listening activity (whether and when you played an episode, and when you last opened the app)To pause making episodes for people who've stopped listening, which keeps the service affordable.
Subscription status (free trial, active or ended, and the renewal date)To know whether to make your episode. Payments are handled entirely by Apple – we never see your card or payment details.
Basic technical information (such as app version and error details, kept by our hosting providers in their normal service logs)To keep the service running and fix problems.

Topics are checked automatically to keep the show suitable for a general audience; topics that aren't allowed (such as adult content) are skipped.

Your morning reminder setting (on/off and the time) is stored only on your phone.

How your episode is made

Each night, our system searches recent news for your topics, uses AI to write a two-host script based on those news sources, checks the script against the sources, and uses AI voices to turn it into audio. The hosts, Maya and Jordan, are AI voices, not real people. Episodes can contain mistakes, so each one lists its sources.

How long we keep it

  • Episode audio, transcripts and source lists: today's and yesterday's only. Older ones are deleted automatically every night.
  • A small record of each episode (date, length, whether it was played, and what it cost us to make – no audio or text): 90 days, then deleted.
  • Shared news research: deleted after one day.
  • Your account and topics: until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your topics anytime in the app.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, topics, episodes and audio. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription – cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Notifications: turn the morning reminder off in the app, or in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Access or questions: email us (below) to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights under local law; we'll honor them.

Security

Your data is stored with our providers using encryption in transit, and each person can access only their own episodes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and keep only what we need.

Children

Daily Spark is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and let you know in the app when appropriate.
